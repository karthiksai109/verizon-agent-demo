# Onboarding and activation policy

New accounts, port-ins, and first-time activation at Sample Telecom Co.

## Number port-in

- Porting requires the old carrier account number, PIN, and billing name exactly as they appear on the old bill.
- Ports complete in minutes for wireless-to-wireless, up to 24 hours for landline/VoIP numbers.
- Do not cancel the old service manually; the port cancels it automatically.

## Account activation

- New lines activate when the SIM/eSIM is installed and the device connects to the network.
- First bills are higher: they include a prorated partial month plus the first full month in advance, plus one-time activation fees.
- The $35 per-line activation fee is waived for online orders.

## First-30-days checklist

- Enable AutoPay within 30 days to keep promotional pricing that requires it.
- Verify trade-in shipment within 30 days or trade-in credits are cancelled and the device charged at retail.

## Cancellation

- New wireless service can be cancelled within 14 days (buyer's remorse window) with a $50 restocking fee where applicable.
- Home internet cancellation requires equipment return within 30 days to avoid a non-return equipment charge.
