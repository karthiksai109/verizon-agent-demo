# General account FAQ

Cross-topic account and app questions at Sample Telecom Co.

## Account access

- Account owners can add up to 4 account managers; managers can pay bills but not change plans.
- Online accounts lock after 5 failed sign-in attempts for 24 hours, or can be unlocked by SMS verification.

## App and self-service

- Most account tasks (pay bill, change plan, manage devices, track orders) are self-service in the account app.
- Order tracking shows exceptions the same day they occur; carriers update scans every 6-12 hours.

## Privacy and data

- Call records are available online for 18 months; detailed billing PDFs for 7 years.
- Marketing data sharing preferences are managed under Privacy settings and apply within 48 hours.

## Contacting support

- Chat support is 24/7; phone support is 7am-11pm local time; store appointments can be booked in the app.
- Social media support can troubleshoot but cannot make account changes for security reasons.

## Escalations

- Any conversation can be escalated to a human specialist on request; unresolved escalations generate a supervisor callback within 2 business days.
