# Billing and payments policy

Applies to postpaid wireless and Fios accounts at Sample Telecom Co.

## Understanding your bill

- Bills are generated on the account's cycle date and cover the upcoming month of service (advance billing) plus any prior overages.
- AutoPay discount is $10 per line when enrolled with a debit card or bank account; credit cards do not qualify.
- One-time charges (device upgrades, late fees, activation fees) appear under "One-time charges and credits".

## Disputing a charge

- Report billing errors within 60 days of the statement date.
- A provisional credit is issued while a disputed charge is investigated if the amount exceeds $50.
- Taxes and regulatory fees are government-mandated and cannot be waived.

## Payment arrangements

- Payment extensions up to 14 days are available to accounts with fewer than 3 extensions in the last 12 months.
- A payment arrangement suspends late fees but does not restore service if the account is already suspended.

## Refunds

- Refunds for service outages exceeding 24 consecutive hours are prorated daily.
- Refund method follows the original payment method; bank refunds take 5-7 business days.
