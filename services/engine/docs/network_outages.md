# Network troubleshooting policy

For wireless coverage, outages, and speed issues at Sample Telecom Co.

## Outage handling

- Confirmed area outages are credited automatically after resolution; no customer action needed.
- Crews repair fiber cuts within 24-48 hours; wireless tower issues are prioritized by affected customer count.
- A zip code can be checked for active incidents before any device troubleshooting begins.

## Slow speeds

- Run a speed test on a wired connection before opening a tickets; wireless results are not used for SLA tickets.
- Deprioritization may apply after 50GB of premium data on unlimited plans during congestion.
- Restarting the router resolves roughly 40% of reported slow-speed cases.

## 5G coverage questions

- 5G Ultra Wideband requires a 5G-capable device and the correct plan tier.
- Indoor signal issues can often be resolved by enabling Wi-Fi Calling in device settings.

## Escalation

- Speed tickets older than 72 hours without resolution escalate to tier-2 network engineering automatically.
