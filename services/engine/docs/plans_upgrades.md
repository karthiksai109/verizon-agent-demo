# Plans and upgrades policy

Plan selection, upgrades, and promotions at Sample Telecom Co.

## Plan changes

- Plan changes take effect at the start of the next billing cycle unless requested as immediate (prorated).
- Downgrading a plan in the middle of a device payment agreement does not affect the device installments.

## Device upgrades

- Upgrade eligibility requires 50% of the device payment agreement to be paid, or 12 months elapsed.
- Trade-in values are quoted at order time and credited over 24-36 months as bill credits.

## Promotions

- Promotional pricing stacks only where explicitly stated; loyalty discounts and trade-in credits do not stack.
- New-line promotions require port-in from an eligible carrier and activation within 30 days.

## International

- Unlimited Ultimate plans include 210+ destination international data; other plans use TravelPass at $12/day.
- TravelPass days reset at midnight Eastern Time.
