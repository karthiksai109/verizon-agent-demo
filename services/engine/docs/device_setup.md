# Device setup and support policy

Phones, tablets, hotspots, and eSIM at Sample Telecom Co.

## eSIM and SIM

- eSIM activation requires the device to be on Wi-Fi and takes under 10 minutes.
- Moving a number to a new phone with eSIM is self-service from the account app under Devices > Manage.
- A physical SIM replacement ships free to the account address of record.

## Common device issues

- A forced restart (hold power + volume down for 10 seconds) resolves most unresponsive-screen reports.
- Visual Voicemail requires iOS carrier settings 52.0 or later / Android My-Services app 9.x.
- Battery swelling is a safety issue: power off, do not charge, and bring the device to a corporate store.

## Warranty

- Manufacturer defects are covered for 12 months; accidental damage requires a protection plan.
- Protection-plan claims have a $99 screen deductible and $229 full-replacement deductible.

## Device unlock

- Devices auto-unlock 60 days after paid-in-full activation.
- Deployed military personnel can request immediate unlock with orders.
